Sounds bundled with EventSlide (spec 12, "a sound on each new photo")

web/public/sounds/chime.mp3   Carillon
web/public/sounds/bell.mp3    Cloche
web/public/sounds/drop.mp3    Goutte
web/public/sounds/pop.mp3     Pop

Made for EventSlide by its maintainers, from nothing: each file is a sum of sine waves under
exponential envelopes, computed by tools/sounds/generate.mjs and encoded to mono 44.1 kHz
64 kbit/s mp3 by ffmpeg. No sample, no recording and no third-party sound is used, so there is
nothing to attribute.
