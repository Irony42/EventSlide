## GitHub release: body

> **EventSlide 3.4 makes the evening easier to run, and tells everyone what is going on.**
>
> - **Guests are told when to look up.** When the host validates a photo, the guest's phone says it is
>   about to go on the screen. A photo just published takes the next slide instead of waiting for a lap,
>   and a guest's first photo carries « Première photo de Julie ».
> - **Fewer traps on the phone.** Leaving the upload screen mid-send no longer loses photos, the states
>   say what is happening (ready, waiting for the network, sent), and a closed evening is a calm thank-you.
> - **A wall that behaves.** It keeps the screen awake, is calm before and after the evening, and `Q`
>   (not Escape) hides the QR.
> - **Hosts and moderators.** Archiving and opening ask first, « Après la soirée » leads the page once
>   it is over, Z undoes a publication, the phone console can take a photo off the wall, and the buttons
>   on a photo are the ones its status allows.
> - **One vocabulary** in the five languages: « la soirée », « album », « Code de la soirée »,
>   « Terminer la soirée ».
>
> **This is a minor release and breaks nothing.** There is no migration and nothing to change in `.env`.
> Worth knowing: a guest's phone now reads `GET /photos/mine` about every nine seconds while a photo
> waits for validation, and that route has no rate limit, so **watch the load at your first large event**;
> three fields are added to existing responses; and the words on every screen changed. Read "Behaviour
> changes to check before upgrading" in the
> [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.4.0/CHANGELOG.md) and
> [Upgrading to 3.4](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.4.0/docs/UPGRADING.md#upgrading-to-34).
