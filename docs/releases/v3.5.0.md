## GitHub release: body

> **EventSlide 3.5 gives guests a voice, hosts something to keep and print, and the room a highlight reel.**
>
> - **A written guestbook.** Guests leave a short message from their phone; the host moderates it like a
>   photograph, the wall reads the published ones aloud between photographs, and the album ZIP keeps them.
> - **Print and brand.** Table cards and posters with the QR (black or in the event's colour, with the logo
>   at its centre if you want), a welcome message and the host's logo on the join page and the empty wall.
> - **A PDF photo book** of the published album, and **a best-of**: star your favourites, download them as
>   a ZIP, or ask for a highlight video.
> - **Event statistics**: sends, guests, reactions, top contributors, and how many times the album link was
>   opened after the evening (counted per day, with no identity).
> - **For operators**: a per-client switch for photo books, and the take-down of a single guestbook message.
>
> **This is a minor release and breaks nothing, but it is bigger than usual.** It applies six migrations
> (018 to 023, the last one rebuilding the append-only audit table), so take the archive first; **new events
> open with the guestbook on** while existing ones keep it off; `pdfkit` and four fonts are new; finished
> books and montages live outside the backups, so budget the disk; and twelve optional settings (`BOOK_*`,
> `REEL_*`, `MAX_QUEUED_REELS`, `NEW_CLIENT_BOOKS_ALLOWED`) all have defaults. Read "Behaviour changes to
> check before upgrading" in the
> [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.5.0/CHANGELOG.md) and
> [Upgrading to 3.5](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.5.0/docs/UPGRADING.md#upgrading-to-35).
