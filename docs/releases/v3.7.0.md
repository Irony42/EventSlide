## GitHub release: body

> **EventSlide 3.7 lets guests trim a video that is too long, moves the guestbook into the moderation console and
> shows photographs whole on the wall.**
>
> - **Trim a long video on the phone.** A recording over the limit no longer ends in a refusal: the guest picks
>   the part to keep, the phone cuts it without re-encoding and sends only that.
> - **Guestbook messages in the console.** A Photos | Messages switch, on the desktop and on the phone console,
>   with the same decisions and the same undo as for photographs.
> - **A settings drawer and an icon bar on the wall**, with visual controls for the layout, the rhythm, the
>   invitation card and the sound.
> - **Photographs shown whole.** A portrait photo full screen is no longer cropped, and the mosaic keeps at
>   least 70 % of every photo.
>
> **This is a minor release and breaks nothing.** Worth knowing: one migration (025, two nullable columns,
> forward-only, so take the archive first); one new dependency, `mp4box`, in a separate chunk fetched only when
> a guest trims a video; the mosaic layout looks different (six equal cells, no large tile); and the guestbook
> card on the event page is now a summary with a link to the console. Read "Behaviour changes to check before
> upgrading" in the
> [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.7.0/CHANGELOG.md) and
> [Upgrading to 3.7](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.7.0/docs/UPGRADING.md#upgrading-to-37).
