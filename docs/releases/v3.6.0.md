## GitHub release: body

> **EventSlide 3.6 puts the controls on the wall, and gives the room a challenge, a counter and a sound.**
>
> - **A settings panel on the projector.** The owner, signed in on the projector machine, opens it with `R`:
>   layout and rhythm, new photos first, tilt, send time, the invitation card's size, corner and visibility,
>   the sound, and a challenge to launch.
> - **A timed photo challenge.** A host launches a mission for 15 seconds to 30 minutes; the wall and the
>   guests' phones count down together, and the console shows how many photographs answered.
> - **A souvenir counter, a new-photo signal and a sound** for each new photograph, and **a wall that
>   alternates** with one large photograph, in step on every projector.
>
> **This is a minor release and breaks nothing, and nothing changes on an existing event until its host acts**:
> every new setting defaults to today's behaviour, and the sound is off. Worth knowing: one migration (024,
> a new table, forward-only, so take the archive first); the wall's read gains public fields; a guest's phone
> reads `GET /missions/mine` every twenty seconds while the event has missions, so **watch the load at your
> first large event**; the panel needs the owner signed in on the projector; and a screen plays sound only
> after a click or the `S` key. Read "Behaviour changes to check before upgrading" in the
> [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.6.0/CHANGELOG.md) and
> [Upgrading to 3.6](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.6.0/docs/UPGRADING.md#upgrading-to-36).
