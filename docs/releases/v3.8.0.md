## GitHub release: body

> **EventSlide 3.8 gives the photo challenge its own text, and lets a guest's upload pane fold out of the way.**
>
> - **A challenge with its own text.** The host types it (60 characters, with ready-made ideas and the last
>   texts) and needs no mission. A guest answers by leaving "Je participe au défi" checked, which it is by
>   default.
> - **A collapsible upload pane** on the guest's phone: a bar of at most 14 % of the screen, the selection kept.
> - **Fixes on the wall**: the arrow keys step through photographs and leave a guestbook card, a running
>   challenge stays above the cards, the drawer's switches read on and off at a glance, and the mixed wall
>   changes phase at every boundary.
>
> **This is a minor release and breaks nothing.** Worth knowing: one migration (026, one nullable column on
> `photos`, forward-only, so take the archive first); a challenge running during the upgrade loses the answers
> sent before it; every guest's phone now reads `GET /missions/mine` every twenty seconds on every event,
> missions or not, so **watch the load at your first large event**; and deleting a mission no longer stops a
> challenge. Read "Behaviour changes to check before upgrading" in the
> [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.8.0/CHANGELOG.md) and
> [Upgrading to 3.8](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.8.0/docs/UPGRADING.md#upgrading-to-38).
