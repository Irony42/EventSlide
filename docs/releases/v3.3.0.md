## GitHub release: body

> **EventSlide 3.3 gives guests and operators the controls a public service needs.**
>
> - **A guest can erase everything they sent**, from their phone, at once: photos, clips, reactions and
>   their name leave the wall, the feed and the host's pages. It works even after the host revoked them.
> - **Remove without viewing.** On a hosted box, the operator can take down a photo or a whole event, and
>   hold an event's purge to preserve evidence, from a new "Retrait" page that never shows the content.
> - **Accounts.** A host can delete their own account, and the proof that they accepted the terms is kept
>   on the account.
> - **Clearer privacy notice**, which now names the guests' feed and the new erase button; every guest is
>   shown it once more.
> - The invisible glass material is retired.
>
> **This is a minor release and breaks nothing**, but it applies three migrations and lowers the default
> audit retention to 365 days: **a box that never set `AUDIT_RETENTION_DAYS` deletes audit lines older than a
> year after the upgrade.** Read "Behaviour changes to check before upgrading" in the
> [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.3.0/CHANGELOG.md) and
> [Upgrading to 3.3](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.3.0/docs/UPGRADING.md#upgrading-to-33).
