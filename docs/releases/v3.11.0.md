## GitHub release: body

> **EventSlide 3.11 files each photo under the mission it was picked for, lets a video clip join a mission, and
> stops a photo awaiting approval from looking like a bug.**
>
> - **Each photo keeps its mission.** Photos picked one after the other and sent in one go were all filed under
>   the last mission selected; each is now filed under the one selected when it was picked. "Vos envois" labels
>   every photo with its mission ("Mission : …"), or "Défi" when it answers a challenge.
> - **A video clip can be filed under a mission**, like a photo. It counts toward the mission's progress on the
>   wall once published and shows the same label. The clip upload endpoint takes an optional `missionId`.
> - **A photo awaiting the host's approval shows in black and white**, slightly faded, instead of a colour
>   negative that guests took for a display bug. It takes its colours when published.
> - **API**: `GET /api/events/:slug/photos/mine` returns `missionPrompt` and `answeredChallenge`.
>
> **This is a minor release, with one migration.** Migration 027 (`clip_jobs.mission_id`) is applied
> automatically at start-up; nothing to set. Back up first, as for any upgrade, since migrations only go
> forward: pull the image and restart. See the
> [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.11.0/CHANGELOG.md).
