## GitHub release: body

> **EventSlide 3.2 keeps a full room sending.**
>
> - **Limits per guest, not per Wi-Fi.** Upload and reaction limits now count each guest of an event,
>   so a venue whose guests share one public address no longer shares twelve sends a minute. A separate,
>   generous ceiling per address still stops a flood, and the join limit defaults to 200 a minute.
> - **One event cannot take every upload slot.** Slots are taken per event and only once the guest is
>   identified, and an upload that stalls gives its slot back after ten minutes.
> - **A tidier guest screen.** The composer folds its caption and video options under labelled
>   buttons, a sent batch folds into one summary line, and the upload pane stands out from the list
>   scrolling under it.
>
> **This is a minor release and breaks nothing.** There is no migration and nothing to change in
> `.env`. If you set an upload, reaction or join limit, read "Behaviour changes to check before
> upgrading" in the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.2.0/CHANGELOG.md) and
> [Upgrading to 3.2](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.2.0/docs/UPGRADING.md#upgrading-to-32).
