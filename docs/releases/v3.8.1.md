## GitHub release: body

> **EventSlide 3.8.1 fixes the wall's cursor, the guest upload pane's fold, and polishes the video trim screen.**
>
> - **The wall's cursor stays visible** while the owner uses the settings drawer: moves over the drawer were
>   swallowed, so the cursor, the bar and the sound icon hid while the mouse was moving.
> - **The guest upload pane folds only from its chevron.** The fold on scroll that 3.8.0 added is removed.
> - **The trim screen** has a play/pause icon button, and dragging a handle shows the video frame at that
>   position.
>
> **This is a patch release.** No migration, no new environment variable, nothing to change before upgrading:
> pull the image and restart. See the
> [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.8.1/CHANGELOG.md).
