## GitHub release: body

> **EventSlide 3.1 gives every evening a look of its own.**
>
> - **Ambiances.** An event now wears an ambiance: the Guinguette (the new look of the whole product,
>   the console included), Riviera, Champs d'or, Bivouac, Cabaret and Noces, each with its own
>   surfaces, faces, ornaments and photograph. The host chooses in the event's settings.
> - **« Sur mesure ».** Or the host makes their own: light or dark, a ground hue, an accent and the faces of one of
>   the six, chosen on a page that shows the real guest screen and the real wall as they will be.
>   Whatever the host picks stays readable.
> - **Your venue, behind the wall.** The host can upload a photograph of their venue to replace the
>   ambiance's own, on the guest's phone and the wall. The file is re-encoded and its location
>   metadata removed.
> - **A clothesline wall layout**, and a console that is easier to find your way around: a way back
>   to your events from every page, navigation tiles on the event page, and sign-in, expiry and dead-end
>   fixes for guests and hosts.
>
> **This is a minor release and breaks nothing.** There is no migration and no new setting. Every screen
> looks different, though: open an event you care about before the evening, and see "Behaviour changes to
> check before upgrading" in the
> [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.1.0/CHANGELOG.md) and
> [Upgrading to 3.1](https://github.com/Irony42/EventSlide/blob/v3.1.0/docs/UPGRADING.md#upgrading-to-31).
> A script that writes an event's theme must now send `ambiance`.
